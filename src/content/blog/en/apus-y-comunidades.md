In Andean cosmology, an Apu is not merely a geological landform; it is a sentient mountain presence. One does not 'conquer' it. One walks in humility, asking permission with every step.

## Asking Permission Is Not Decorative

In the Q'ero highlands and around Waqrapukara, local communal authorities decide whether an outside group enters, camps, and participates in rituals. We coordinate every visit months in advance. If a village pauses visits due to seasonal harvest or community assembly, we reschedule without debate.

## Pace Governs Safety

At high elevation, the slowest hiker establishes the group tempo. Our mountain guides carry the professional duty to descend any traveler showing early signs of acute mountain sickness.
