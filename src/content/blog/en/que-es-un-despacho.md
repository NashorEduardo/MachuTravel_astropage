In the Andes, the despacho is a sacred offering bundle. Crafted carefully with seeds, flowers, sugar, and coca leaves, it represents a conscious breath of prayer returned to Pachamama (Mother Earth) and the Apus (protective mountain spirits). The guiding principle is Ayni: sacred reciprocity.

## How It Unfolds in Our Small Groups

A recognized local Paqo master leads the gathering. The group is intimate. Each traveler gently blows an intention into three coca leaves (kintu), either aloud or in silent reflection. There are no theatrical costumes or canned music. If coca is present, it is an essential sacred sacrament; no one is pressured to chew it.

## What It Is Not

It is not a quick commercial show or magical fix. Photographs are held back during sacred invocations. Those wishing to simply observe in quiet contemplation are warmly welcomed.
