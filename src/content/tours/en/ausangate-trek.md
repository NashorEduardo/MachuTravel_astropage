Rising to 6,384 meters, Ausangate is Cusco's highest and most revered peak. Here, high-altitude trekking feels raw, majestic, and deeply humbling.

At Machupicchu Beyond, we travel in small, tight-knit groups, prioritizing health, mountain ethics, and fair wages for the high-Andean pastoral families of Pacchanta.
