The Classic Inca Trail is not just a hike; it is an ancestral pilgrimage engineered by the Incan empire to connect their imperial capital with the sanctuary of Machu Picchu.

At Machupicchu Beyond, we walk these royal stones with reverence and with an uncompromising commitment to the dignity and welfare of our Andean porters and their families.
