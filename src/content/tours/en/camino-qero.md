The Q'ero Nation is not an ethnographic display; it is a living civilization of highland shepherds who have tended an unbroken relationship with nature for five centuries.

Machupicchu Beyond visits Q'ero through the ancestral law of Ayni: ensuring fair remuneration and supporting educational projects for rural school children in Paucartambo.
