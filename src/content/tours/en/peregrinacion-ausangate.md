Apu Ausangate is the most revered sacred mountain in southern Peru. To circle its base on foot with quiet presence transforms trekking into an inner journey of stillness.

At Machupicchu Beyond, we uphold technical excellence while protecting the land and fairly supporting the pastoral communities of Pacchanta.
