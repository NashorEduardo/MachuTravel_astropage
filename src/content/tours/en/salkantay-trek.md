Named by National Geographic as one of the world's top treks, the Salkantay route connects rugged Andean glaciers with fertile Amazonian cloud forests in just five days.

At Machupicchu Beyond, we conduct this expedition with certified mountain leaders, ensuring fair compensation for our horsemen and supporting rural education in the highland hamlets we pass through.
