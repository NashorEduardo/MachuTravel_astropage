Perched at 4,140 meters, Waqrapukara resembles a colossal horned crown towering over a 1,000-meter drop into the roaring Apurímac River.

At Machupicchu Beyond, we reject rushed mass tourism. We partner with local peasant families, dine at their tables, and enter the sanctuary in reverent quiet before the world wakes up.
