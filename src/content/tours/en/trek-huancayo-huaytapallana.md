Huaytapallana ('The Place Where Flowers Are Gathered') has served as the spiritual beacon of the Central Andes for millennia.

Machupicchu Beyond brings conscious travelers to this lesser-known wilderness, fostering awareness of tropical glacier conservation and directly empowering local peasant stewards.
